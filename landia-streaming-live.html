Landia Streaming Live

The live audiovisual branch of Landia’s Productions: a responsible production environment for education, creative broadcasting, interviews, community programming and authorized real-time documentation.

Production conceptOBS-readyMultiplatformPrivacy-separated

Purpose

Landia Streaming Live brings together cameras, microphones, live graphics, remote guests and recorded media into one production workflow. It is designed for public-facing content while maintaining a strict boundary between broadcast material and confidential documentation.

01

Live studio

Multi-source production using phones, computers, microphones, scenes, overlays and recording.

02

Educational channel

Mental-health education, community conversations, technology explainers and accessible public-interest programming.

03

Creative broadcast

Music, performance, Landia characters, interviews and audiovisual storytelling can share the same production infrastructure.

Production flow

Prepare

Define purpose, participants, consent and scene plan.

Produce

Mix audio, video, graphics and remote sources.

Broadcast

Send approved content to selected public channels.

Archive

Keep masters, captions and publication records.

Safety by design

Public ≠ confidential

Evidence or private records are never automatically routed into the live program.

Consent and context

People appearing in planned content should understand the intended audience and use.

Human moderation

AI can assist captions, translation and summaries, but humans control what goes live.

Accessible output

Captions, transcripts, readable graphics and multilingual versions are part of the design.

Studio architecture

INPUT

Cameras & audio

Phones, webcams, lavalier microphones, music sources and remote contributors.

CONTROL

Production console

OBS or equivalent software for scenes, audio routing, overlays, recording and stream control.

OUTPUT

Live + archive

Authorized streaming platforms, local master recordings and edited follow-up content.
