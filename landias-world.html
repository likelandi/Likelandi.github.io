Landia’s World

The creative universe at the heart of Landia’s Productions: original characters, books, music, performance, visual culture and multimedia storytelling connected by one authorial identity.

The universe

Landia’s World is designed as a transmedia setting rather than a single product. A character can begin in writing, appear in visual art or performance, move into music or streaming, and later become part of a larger narrative while retaining a clear connection to the JPFR creative catalog.
