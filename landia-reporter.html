LANDIA'S PRODUCTIONS · PROJECT

Landia Reporter

A visual and documentary reporting concept designed to record events responsibly, organize chronology, preserve original material and support clear communication.

What it does

Structured notes, audiovisual records, timestamps, multilingual transcription, summaries and responsible archival workflows.

Principles

Consent where applicable, privacy, dignity, human review, lawful documentation and a clear distinction between observation, hypothesis and conclusion.

Connection

Designed to connect with Landia Streaming Live, Evidence Creator and Landia Undercover without publishing confidential material automatically.
