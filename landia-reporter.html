Landia Reporter

A documentary reporting system for transforming verified material into clear chronology, multilingual narratives and responsible public or institutional communication.

Editorial conceptMultilingualHuman-reviewedSource-aware

Purpose

Landia Reporter sits between raw material and communication. It organizes dates, sources, audiovisual records and contextual notes into understandable reports while keeping uncertainty visible and confidential material protected.

01

Chronology builder

Turn scattered notes and media into a dated sequence with source references.

02

Multilingual newsroom

Assist transcription, translation, summarization and preparation of Spanish/English reporting.

03

Audience-specific output

Prepare different versions for public communication, professional review or institutional submission.

Editorial workflow

Collect

Gather reviewed facts and source references.

Structure

Build chronology and identify gaps.

Draft

Explain what is known, unknown and alleged.

Review

Human-check privacy, accuracy and tone.

Reporting standard

Facts are labeled as facts

Claims should be backed by identifiable supporting material.

Hypotheses remain hypotheses

Unverified explanations are not presented as established events.

Privacy is editorial

Names, images and personal data can be redacted when disclosure is unnecessary.

Corrections remain possible

Reports should retain version history and allow new evidence to change conclusions.

LANDI REPORTER / CREATIVE PRODUCTION

Stories ready for people, platforms and partners.

Alongside responsible documentation, Landi Reporter develops short-form editorial and audiovisual formats for cultural projects, community organizations and independent creators. Each proposal is scoped individually; this is a portfolio and collaboration invitation, not a claim of an active newsroom or guaranteed service.

FORMAT 01

FLASH / 15–30 sec

Concise visual introductions, announcements and cultural highlights, planned around authorized source material.

FORMAT 02

REPORTER / 45–90 sec

Structured mini-reports, interviews or explainers with script, edit plan and optional bilingual subtitles.

FORMAT 03

HOT REPORT / 1–3 min

Longer context-rich stories, with explicit fact-checking, rights review and human editorial approval.
