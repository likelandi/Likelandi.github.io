Landia Undercover

A privacy-first framework for preserving, organizing and reviewing sensitive material without turning uncertainty into fact or confidential information into public content.

Concept & architectureHuman-reviewedPrivacy-firstMultiplatform vision

Purpose

Landia Undercover is designed as the protected documentation layer of the Landia ecosystem. Its role is to preserve original material, organize context, separate observations from hypotheses, and prepare information for appropriate professional, institutional or legal review.

01

Secure intake

Collect files, notes, photos, video, audio and contextual metadata while preserving originals.

02

Structured review

Build chronology, classify source quality and keep verified facts distinct from allegations or interpretations.

03

Controlled disclosure

Prepare selected, redacted material for authorized recipients instead of indiscriminate publication.

Core workflow

Capture

Preserve originals and context.

Protect

Apply access controls and backups.

Review

Human-check chronology and claims.

Share carefully

Release only what is appropriate.

Ethics & safeguards

No automatic accusation

The system should never infer guilt, conspiracy or authorship from ambiguous material.

Privacy by default

Sensitive records remain private unless there is a clear reason and authority to share them.

Originals preserved

Analysis is performed on copies where possible, retaining provenance and integrity.

Human decision-making

AI may assist with transcription, indexing, translation and redaction, not final judgment.
