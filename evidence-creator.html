Evidence Creator

A workflow concept for turning original digital material into organized, reviewable documentation while protecting integrity and context.

Preserve

Keep original files intact, retain metadata where available and create documented copies for analysis.

Verify

Use hashes, timestamps, provenance and structured logs to make changes and handling easier to audit.

Prepare

Create chronological reports and evidence indexes for appropriate professional, institutional or legal review.
